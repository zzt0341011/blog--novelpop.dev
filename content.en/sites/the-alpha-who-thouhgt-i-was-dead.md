# The Alpha Who Thought I Was Dead: A Heart-Wrenching Werewolf Romance

Looking for a short, emotional werewolf romance that hits hard on grief, obsession, and second chances? **The Alpha Who Thought I Was Dead** (also known as *My Alpha Thought I Died Three Years Ago*) delivers exactly that.

## Story Overview

Three years ago, the Alpha’s fated Luna was driven to the edge of a cliff by an enemy pack and forced to fall. Everyone believed she was dead.

The Alpha lost his mind.  
In the first year, hatred kept him alive — he wiped out the entire enemy pack.  
In the second year, he planted lilies (her favorite flower) across the entire territory.  
By the third year, a strange illness was killing him: sudden fevers, uncontrollable coughing of blood. No healer could save him. Grief was eating him alive.

Then a new healer arrives, half her face hidden behind a white mask.

The moment the Alpha sees her — the way she breathes, the quiet in her eyes — something inside him cracks open. His soul already knows her name.

She is Lily.  
His Lily.

## Why Readers Love It

- Classic **presumed dead + Alpha’s regret** trope done with strong emotion
